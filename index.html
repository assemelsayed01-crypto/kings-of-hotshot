Who Is KHB?

Since 2017, Kings of Hotshots & Boxtrucks has specialized in light shipments that pay well — helping over 650 owner-operators build real income through our lease-on and dispatch programs. Founded and operated out of North Texas by a former OTR driver, we built KHB to be the kind of company we wished we'd had on the road: honest rates, no forced dispatch, and a team that actually answers the phone.
